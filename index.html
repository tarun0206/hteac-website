OUR ORIGIN & JOURNEY

Serving with Pride Since 1973

Our story began in 1973 when Sh. B.K. Singh started the tea journey by personally procuring leaves from the finest estates of Darjeeling, North Bengal, Assam, Sonajuli, Arunachal Pradesh, and Kerala Munnar.

Embraced and expanded by his son, Founder Mr. M.K. Singh, Hindustan Tea established state-of-the-art precision manufacturing to make pure, authentic tea "Everyone's Cup of Tea" across India.
