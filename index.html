♦ OUR PROVENANCE ♦

A Sacred Craft Since 1973

ASSAM 26.20°N · SONAJULI 26.83°N · UPPER BENGAL 26.72°N · NILGIRIS 11.41°N

Our story began in 1973 when Sh. B.K. Singh embarked on a mission to bring pure, unadulterated whole-leaf tea from India's most revered mountain slopes straight to discerning teacups.

Carried forward and modernized by Founder Mr. M.K. Singh, Hindustan Tea Company established hygienic state-of-the-art precision blending while honoring century-old orthodox tea rituals — creating what has truly become "Everyone's Cup of Tea".
