Shipping Policy

Information regarding dispatch timelines, freight handling, and nationwide delivery of artisan tea consignments by Hindustan Tea Company Private Limited.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

All tea allocations and retail packages are inspected, hermetically sealed, and dispatched from our primary facility in Jhajjar, Haryana within 24 to 48 business hours following order confirmation.

2. Delivery Timelines Across India

  • Delhi NCR & Northern Region: 2 to 4 business days.
  • Metropolitan Centers (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai): 3 to 5 business days.
  • Rest of India: 5 to 7 business days depending on regional logistics accessibility.

3. Shipment Tracking & Packaging Integrity

Once your consignment has been handed over to our verified national courier partners, an automated notification containing tracking credentials will be shared via email and SMS. Every package is shipped in multi-barrier moisture-lock protective cartons to ensure garden-fresh arrival.
