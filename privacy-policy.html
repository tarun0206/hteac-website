Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1st September 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how Hindustan Tea Company Private Limited collects, utilizes, and safeguards your information when you visit or submit details through www.hindustantea.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you request an invitation, register for tasting updates, or contact our customer concierge, we may collect:

  • Personal Contact Data: Name, email address, contact telephone number, and postal communication address.
  • Transactional Details: Inquiry history, feedback, tasting allocation preferences, and correspondence.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and timestamp of interaction, used strictly to maintain website security and service reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling your tasting requests, inquiries, and customer care assistance.
  • Communicating archival blend releases, updates, and corporate announcements.
  • Ensuring regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and operational integrity.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.

3. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard encryption, SSL/TLS protocols, and secure administrative controls to protect personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set forth in this policy or to satisfy statutory obligations under applicable Indian laws.
